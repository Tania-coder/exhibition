The story

This is Rainer Fetting: a painter who keeps meeting himself on the canvas.

Rainer Fetting is a German painter. His language is the expressive figure: strong colour, energetic brushwork, and a human body in the middle of the canvas.

He works in cycles, and very often they are self-portraits. Faces, men, portraits, even Bowie. And since 1982, one subject keeps returning: the modern human condition.

One name follows him everywhere: Van Gogh. Fetting painted him again and again, even in the New York subway, with Gauguin standing behind him like a shadow. A critic in 1986 wrote that he identifies with Vincent down to the paint itself.

Since 1983, New York is his main line. Not the postcard city, but a city seen from the very edge, by an eternal newcomer who never fully arrives.

But painting is not all. Since 1986 he makes bronze sculptures, and the big shows in 2008 and 2010 gave them space. He has also photographed since the early eighties. And in 1985 his paintings appeared in the film To Live and Die in L.A., where he plays a priest.
