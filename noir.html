The story

1982. Thierry Noir is 23. He arrives in West Berlin with two suitcases and no plan.

His window faces the Wall. Everyone knows you cannot paint on it. But he is French, a foreigner. In 1984, he starts.

Not to make it beautiful. To make it ridiculous, and take away the fear.

Heads. Big eyes, big lips, one line, three colors, painted fast while guards watch. Two ideas: good and bad, East and West.

After 1989 he paints the East side, through holes in the concrete, with soldiers watching.

The hand gives the idea to the head. Work, every day.
