The story

Elvira Bach, a German painter from Berlin. Her one theme: women and femininity. Look closely and you notice that all the faces she paints look alike. That is not a coincidence. They are, in essence, self-portraits.

Bright, loud female figures: she painted them especially since the late 1990s. Critics called it too repetitive, too decorative. Her answer, again and again: I painted my life. I always started from myself.

Her best-known series is the kitchen divas. An art historian describes such a heroine like this: a deep neckline, stiletto heels, and a cigarette in a strawberry mouth.

She doesn't only paint. She makes bronze sculptures, ceramics, and sculptures in Murano glass. And the glass has a story: she began her path as a glassblower's apprentice. The material she started with came back to her.

In 2025, Galerie Friese in Berlin showed So rot, so rot. The focus was on early works from the 1970s and 80s, plus new ceramics made in the summer of 2025. Softer, less poster-like than what we link to her name today.
